## CUT 15 — 에너지 폭발 (00:20–00:21)

**장면**: 천족의 몸에서 극강의 황금 에너지가 폭발. 흰색-금색 코어에서 방사상 확산.

**촬영**: 14mm 극광각, 매우 로우앵글, 카메라 쉐이크

**조명**: 2800K 흰색-금색, 모든 방향에서 방사

**음향**: 강렬한 충격음 SFX + 오케스트라 절정

```
SCENE CONTEXT
The transformed warrior stands at the center of a massive golden energy explosion. 
The figure is positioned upright, arms outstretched or at sides, at the exact epicenter. 
A shockwave of golden light erupts outward in all directions at extreme intensity.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: particle trails and energy distortion closest to camera, extreme depth of field.
Midground: the warrior figure, silhouetted by the explosion's core.
Background: blown-out golden-white light receding infinitely.
Camera positioned directly below the figure, 8–10 meters away, looking sharply upward.
Light emanates from within the figure outward; no external source.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Frame 0.0s: The warrior stands centered in the frame, explosion beginning to radiate outward from the body.
Composition: extreme low-angle 14mm ultra-wide, the figure occupies center-to-upper frame, 
sky/background fills lower frame and edges (inverted horizon).
All light originates from the figure, no light from above or behind.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 1 second duration, the camera does not cut and does not pan on its own.

OPTICS
Extreme wide-angle 107° FOV (14mm equivalent).
Shot size: full figure + surrounding explosion, perspective distorted by ultra-wide lens.
No drift mid-segment.

CAMERA
Handheld low-angle, camera positioned at ground level or below the figure's feet, 
locked on upward vertical axis pointed at center of chest/core.
Extreme camera shake: tremor amplitude 3–5cm, frequency 15–20 Hz, suggesting impact shockwave.
Operator holds steady on the vertical axis; all motion is tremor, not pan/tilt.

ACTION
0.0s–0.1s: Explosion begins at the figure's core, expanding outward.
0.1s–0.7s: Golden energy radiates and expands in all directions, shockwave visible in air distortion.
Particles accelerate outward at 80–100 km/h, visible as trails and distortion waves.
0.7s–1.0s: Intensity peaks, white-hot core (center of figure) reaches maximum brightness.
Camera shake reaches peak at 0.4s–0.7s, subsides slightly by 1.0s as energy settles to plateau.

PHYSICS
Explosion propagates as a perfect sphere expanding from the figure's center.
Particles accelerate outward with decreasing density gradient — dense near center, sparse at edges.
Air distortion creates visible shockwave fronts radiating as concentric rings.
Contact shadows: ground underneath the figure remains in shadow despite surrounding brightness 
(rim light only from sides and above).
Particle trails follow divergent vectors from the center; no vortex or swirl.

LIGHTING
Single source: white-hot core at the warrior's center (2800K absolute peak).
Light spreads outward in all directions: upward, downward, left, right, forward, backward.
Intensity: absolute maximum — 80–90% of frame blown out to near-white by 0.4s.
Shadows inside the figure's silhouette remain dark (interior remains defined).
Rim lighting: sharp bright edge around the figure's outline against the golden background.

COLOR GRADE
Core temperature (0.0s–0.5s): pure white-hot (2800K, neutral/slightly yellow).
Mid-transition (0.3s–0.7s): white + golden-yellow blend, saturation increasing.
Peak saturation (0.7s–1.0s): saturated golden-yellow (2800K, 100% saturation, hue shift toward amber).
Overall brightness: 90%+ of frame is "bright" by 0.5s, sustained to 1.0s.
Contrast is retained by keeping shadows (inside figure, ground) dark.

STYLE
Hyper-realistic, explosive, cinematic, maximum dynamic range. 
Photographic heavy exposure with retained shadow detail. 
Particle physics rendered with fine detail; no blur or smoothing of the shockwave edges.
Extreme detail in particle trails and air distortion — every wavefront visible.

OUTPUT SETTINGS
16:9 aspect ratio.
Resolution: 4K or higher for detail preservation.
Frame rate: 24 fps real-time (not slow-motion).
Grain: fine natural film grain, minimal added noise.
Anamorphic: no — rectilinear lens distortion only.

POSITIVE LOCKS
— Figure remains centered and upright throughout; no fall, no stagger, no rotation.
— Shockwave expands perfectly radially outward; no direction bias.
— Core remains white-hot (2800K) at the center; background is golden (not orange, not red).
— Camera shake is tremor only; no pan, no tilt, no drift from center-up axis.
— Explosion brightness increases monotonically 0.0s–0.5s, then holds at plateau 0.5s–1.0s.
— Ground shadow remains visible beneath figure throughout.
```

## CUT 19 — 우주적 결집 (00:26–00:27)

**장면**: 흩어진 입자들이 거대한 새(피닉스) 형태로 결집. 날개 형태 명확. 우주적 규모.

**촬영**: 광각 18mm (63°), 정면 아이레벨, 팬 좌→우

**조명**: 역광 + 측면광, 내부에서 발광 (3200K 극포화)

**음향**: 신스 음역대 + 현악기 상승

```
SCENE CONTEXT
Scattered particles of golden light suddenly coalesce into the form of a massive cosmic bird.
The phoenix shape is unmistakable: wings spread wide, body centered, feathers detailed and luminous.
The bird hovers in center-frame, alive with internal golden radiance.
Camera positioned at eye level, mid-distance (20–30 meters), viewing the full bird form.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: individual particle details closest to camera, glowing with internal light.
Midground: the complete bird form, solidified from dissolving particles.
Background: deep blue-golden sky, gradient from gold (lower) to warm twilight (upper).
Light emanates entirely from within the particle cloud; no external source.
Camera on slight rise, neutral position, bird centered in frame.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Frame 0.0s: Particles still slightly dispersed but clearly forming the bird outline.
Composition: 63° observational wide-angle, centered two-shot rule (bird centered, sky fills edges).
By frame 0.5s, the bird form is completely solid and recognizable.
Gaze: the bird's head is turned slightly forward, eyes visible and glowing.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 1 second duration.
Camera motion: slow pan left-to-right starting at 0.2s, 10° total pan over 0.8 seconds (5 km/h pan speed).
Pan motion reveals the depth of the bird's wings.

OPTICS
Wide-angle 63° FOV (28–35mm equivalent).
Shot size: full bird figure (wingspan edge to edge) + surrounding sky (40% frame).
No drift mid-segment.

CAMERA
Static camera height, eye-level axis.
Pan origin: slightly off-center left, moving right in a slow horizontal sweep.
Pan speed: constant 5 km/h, no acceleration or deceleration.
Focus: locked on the bird's body center, wings fall within depth of field.
Operator axis: level horizon, pan only, no tilt.

ACTION
0.0s–0.3s: Particle coalescence completes; bird form solidifies.
0.3s–0.8s: Slow pan left-to-right reveals the full wingspan and feather detail.
0.8s–1.0s: Pan reaches completion on the right side; bird form holds steady.
No subject movement — the bird floats stationary; only camera pans.
Particles within the bird form shimmer with internal light throughout.

PHYSICS
Particle assembly: individual particles drift together from all directions, no collision impact (soft assembly).
Feather detail emerges as particles align into feather patterns; no sharp edges or gaps.
Internal luminosity: golden light emanates uniformly from within the particle cloud, no shadow inside.
Bird mass floats weightlessly; no gravity effect, no downward sag.
Air: clear, no haze or distortion between camera and subject.

LIGHTING
Internal source: particles glow with 3200K golden-white light radiating outward from body.
Rim lighting: sharp golden edge around the bird outline where particles catch back-light from sky.
Backlighting: the sky behind the bird glows warmer (gold transitioning to amber at the horizon).
Exposure: bird interior and sky are balanced; neither blown out nor crushed.
Shadows: none — the bird is self-luminous.

COLOR GRADE
Particle color (0.0s–0.5s): golden-white (3200K, shifting toward pure gold by 0.5s).
Peak saturation (0.5s–1.0s): saturated golden-yellow (90–100% saturation).
Sky gradient: upper sky is cool (5500K twilight blue), transitioning to warm golden-amber at horizon (3200K).
Overall tone: majestic, luminous, warm-dominant.
Contrast: bird interior is bright, sky background is also bright (high-key overall).

STYLE
Hyper-realistic, cosmic, majestic, ethereal. 
Photographic with extreme clarity and color saturation. 
Feather texture rendered in fine detail; each feather strand visible as a composite of particles. 
Internal glow effect is volumetric and convincing (not a flat overlay).

OUTPUT SETTINGS
16:9 aspect ratio.
Resolution: 4K.
Frame rate: 24 fps real-time.
Grain: minimal; clean photographic palette.
Anamorphic: no.

POSITIVE LOCKS
— Bird form remains perfectly centered for first 0.2s, then pans left-to-right only (no tilt, no roll).
— Feather detail remains consistent; no gaps or missing sections.
— Internal glow color stays golden-yellow (not white, not orange).
— Sky color remains distinct from bird (sky is cooler at top, bird is always golden-yellow).
— Pan speed constant at 5 km/h; no acceleration.
— Bird floats weightlessly; no descending or rising motion.
```

## CUT 20 — 날개 펼침 (최종) (00:27–00:28)

**장면**: 거대한 새가 날개를 힘껏 펼친다. 입자 흩어짐. 하늘이 완전히 황금색으로 변함.

**촬영**: 14mm 극광각, 로우앵글, 빠른 회전

**조명**: 모든 방향 역광, 극강의 포화도

**음향**: 강렬한 날개 음 + 오케스트라 절정

```
SCENE CONTEXT
The cosmic bird spreads its wings to their fullest extent in a powerful, final gesture.
Wings expand from center frame outward. Particles scatter violently from the wing expansion.
The bird dominates the frame against a completely golden sky. This is the climactic visual moment.
Camera positioned low below the bird, extremely low angle, looking sharply upward.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: scattered particle trails closest to camera, speed-blurred by wing motion.
Midground: the bird's body and fully-spread wings, silhouetted against golden sky.
Background: completely saturated golden-yellow sky, no gradation (uniform color from horizon to top).
Camera positioned 6–8 meters below the bird, locked on upward axis.
Light is omni-directional, no clear source direction (bird self-illuminates).

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Frame 0.0s: Bird's wings are still 80% furled.
By frame 0.3s: Wings are at maximum spread.
Composition: extreme low-angle 14mm ultra-wide (107° FOV).
Bird fills center-upper frame; sky fills lower frame and sides (reversed perspective due to low angle).
Wings span edge-to-edge of frame by mid-shot (0.4s onward).

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 1 second duration.
Camera motion: rotation (roll) starting at 0.2s, 360° counter-clockwise spin, completing at 0.9s.
No pan or tilt — rotation only, on a vertical axis through the frame center.
Rotation speed accelerates 0.2s–0.5s, holds constant 0.5s–0.9s.

OPTICS
Extreme wide-angle 107° FOV (14mm equivalent).
Shot size: full wingspan edge-to-edge + surrounding sky.
No drift mid-segment. Rotation does not change FOV.

CAMERA
Handheld very low-angle, positioned beneath the bird.
Camera rotates counter-clockwise (roll) around vertical central axis (bird remains in center).
Rotation speed: accelerates from 0 to 180°/second over 0.2s–0.5s, then holds at 180°/second until 0.9s.
Focus: locked on bird body center throughout; wings remain in focus.
Operator: pivot point is at frame center; pure rotational motion, no translation.

ACTION
0.0s–0.3s: Wings unfurl from tucked position, expanding outward and upward at accelerating speed.
0.3s–0.5s: Wing expansion reaches completion. Feather details lock into final position.
0.5s–1.0s: Wings hold fully spread. Scattered particles from the expansion continue their trajectories outward.
Particle motion (independent of bird): 150–200 km/h radial velocity, density highest around wing tips.
Camera roll (independent of subject): counter-clockwise 360° rotation over 0.7 seconds (180°/sec).

PHYSICS
Wing spread: feathers unfold sequentially from body to tips (outer feathers reach full extension last).
Particle scatter: explosive outward spray from the wing motion, no vortex, purely radial.
Particles decelerate slightly as they disperse (acceleration due to wing motion, then coast).
No wing collision with anything; space is clear.
Mass behavior: wings are massive but move swiftly (implicit strength/power).

LIGHTING
Omni-directional 360° backlighting from all directions simultaneously (sun surrounds the bird).
Core intensity: white-hot (2800K) at the bird's center.
Wing edges: rim-lit to glowing white.
Sky: completely blown out to saturated golden-yellow (no darker areas, no shadows).
Exposure: sustained at maximum brightness throughout.

COLOR GRADE
Transition (0.0s–0.4s): golden-yellow brightening and saturating (moving from 80% to 100% saturation).
Peak phase (0.4s–1.0s): pure saturated golden-yellow (100% saturation, 2800K warm tone, maximum brightness).
Sky uniformity: entire background is single golden-yellow hue (no gradient, no variation).
No shadows anywhere in frame.

STYLE
Hyper-realistic, epic, cosmic, transformative. 
Maximum dynamic range photography. 
Feather detail extraordinary (each feather's individual barbs visible). 
Particle physics: every scattered element rendered individually with speed-blur trails.
Rotation effect integrated smoothly; no vertigo, no disorientation (rotation serves the rising energy).

OUTPUT SETTINGS
16:9 aspect ratio.
Resolution: 4K or higher.
Frame rate: 24 fps real-time.
Grain: fine natural film grain.
Anamorphic: no — rectilinear ultra-wide only.
Motion blur: on wings and particles proportional to velocity.

POSITIVE LOCKS
— Bird remains centered through 360° camera rotation.
— Wings spread symmetrically; no tilt or asymmetry.
— Sky color stays golden-yellow throughout (not orange, not white, not amber).
— All feathers remain crisp and detailed even at maximum golden brightness.
— Particles maintain outward radial motion; no inward collapse.
— Camera rotation is purely roll (counter-clockwise); no pan, no tilt.
— Rotation speed: 0°/sec at start, peaks at 180°/sec by 0.5s, holds until 0.9s.
```
